Community Care Practitioners: trained by us, for us.

Our Community Care Practitioners are trained members of our own community who hold space for healing. They're not outsiders learning about our experiences from a textbook. They understand what it means to move through the world as a Black person in Montréal, because they live it too.

This is a paid training — participants receive an honorarium for their time and contributions.

The Community Care Practitioners (CCP) Training program is a collaborative initiative between the Black Healing Centre, Dr. Lisa Ndejuru, and the SHIFT Centre for Social Transformation at Concordia University. This program adopts a decolonial, anti-racist, and community-engaged approach to address issues of anti-Black racism, racial trauma, and community mental health.

It aims to build a network of Afro-identified community care practitioners who acknowledge and validate the lived experiences of Black people. Through a holistic and preventative approach, participants learn to find the root causes of clients' issues, focusing on comprehensive care rather than just treating symptoms.
