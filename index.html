Our mission

Strengthening what already exists within us.

BHC creates and sustains culturally affirming spaces where Black communities in Montréal can heal, connect, and build collective power. We cultivate environments where the resilience, creativity, and leadership within Black communities can thrive.

We provide accessible, community-led healing practices, leadership development, and spaces for dialogue and solidarity: resources that strengthen what already exists, rather than positioning the community as a deficit to be fixed.
